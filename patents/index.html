From a personalized system for preventing drug allergy to the MyData Safe Medication system, this is the record of research turned into inventions. 7 registered, 4 filed.

Information processing server and method for providing a personalized safe medication serviceRegistered

Registered 10-2982457 2026.6.22Filed 10-2023-0009537 2023.1.25Min-Gyu Kang, Chungbuk National University Hospital

An information processing server and method that reads a patient's care and medication records together with their side-effect history and delivers a personalized safe medication service. This is the core claim behind the MyData-based Safe Medication system.

Inventors Min-Gyu Kang · MyData CDSS

Method and apparatus for calculating a personalized probability of an adverse drug reactionUSRegistered

Registered 12,394,529 2025.8.19Filed 18/345,630 Chungbuk National University Hospital

The system identifies a drug from a photo of its package taken with a smartphone camera, takes in the side effects the user reports after taking it, and updates that person's probability of a reaction using incentive and disincentive constants. For combination products it calculates a separate probability for each ingredient, and it calculates blood concentrations to tell symptoms of the illness apart from drug side effects.

Inventors Min-Gyu Kang · Digital Drug Safety Card

(Divisional application) Method and apparatus for probability-based estimation of adverse drug reactionsRegistered

Registered 10-2706000 2024.9.20Filed 10-2024-0046870 2024.4.5Chungbuk National University Hospital

A method and apparatus that learns cumulatively from the drugs a person has taken and from every episode with or without a reaction, then updates the probability of a reaction for each ingredient. It factors in reward and reverse-reward constants, reaction intensity, and the residual blood concentration of earlier drugs to tell symptoms of the illness apart from drug side effects. A divisional application of 10-2020-0187377.

Inventors Min-Gyu Kang · MyData CDSS

Method and apparatus for calculating a personalized probability of an adverse drug reactionRegistered

Registered 10-2656669 2024.4.5Filed 10-2020-0187377 2020.12.30Chungbuk National University Hospital, Min-Gyu Kang

A method and apparatus that learns from a person's own dosing experience and side-effect history and computes, in real time, that individual's probability of a reaction rather than a statistical average. It analyzes combination products ingredient by ingredient, separates symptoms of the illness itself from drug side effects, and grows more accurate as dosing experience accumulates.

Inventors Min-Gyu Kang · Digital Drug Safety Card

Method and apparatus for detecting culprit drugs and providing optimized medication using personalized side-effect dataRegistered

Registered 10-2609482 2023.11.29Filed 10-2022-0122884 2022.9.27Chungbuk National University Hospital, Min-Gyu Kang

Using a personalized side-effect database, this method and apparatus automatically flags the drugs on a prescription that carry a high risk of a reaction and recommends safe alternatives with the same therapeutic effect, producing an optimized prescription. It includes causality assessment with the WHO-UMC and Naranjo scales and a self-learning loop fed by the patient's feedback after each dose.

Inventors Min-Gyu Kang · MyData CDSS

Method for assessing allergy risk from ingredient namesRegistered

Registered 10-2198829 2021.1.6Filed 10-2018-0172931 2018.12.28Chungbuk National University Hospital, Chungbuk National University Industry-Academic Cooperation Foundation

A drug entered by brand name, ingredient name, barcode or QR code is converted to its WHO standard ATC code, and the person's drug allergy risk is graded on three levels: contraindicated, caution, and exception. It breaks combination products down by ingredient and finds other formulations that use the same ingredient, preventing cross-reactive allergic reactions.

Inventors Min-Gyu Kang, Mi-Hye Kim, Yong-Gi Kim · Drug Allergy Alert
