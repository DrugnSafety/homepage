Transcript

myadr.care brings patient records into a structured medication-safety review. This demonstration uses deidentified interface captures.

The broader workflow can normalize FHIR data, organize a medication list, run automated screening, and prepare a draft for clinical review. These upstream steps are workflow context and were not recorded here.

The first real screen is a blank patient-registration form. No patient was entered or saved. The second real screen is the DUR Screening Dataset. Candidate findings appear with source-referenced sentences and evaluation columns for reviewer inspection.

The scores are automated appraisal signals, not validated measures of clinical accuracy. One captured row may contain an ingredient mismatch.
